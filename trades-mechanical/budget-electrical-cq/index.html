Electrical Safety Tips

  • Never overload outlets: Use power boards with built-in surge protectors.
  • Check your smoke alarms: Test them monthly and change batteries yearly.
  • Inspect cords: Replace frayed or damaged power cords immediately.
  • Water and electricity don't mix: Keep appliances away from sinks and baths.
