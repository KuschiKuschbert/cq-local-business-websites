Behind the Scenes

Meet Sally – Founder & Event Visionary

Riviera Yeppoon is the culmination of a decade-long passion for events and culinary hospitality. After successfully managing *A Days Graze Catering* and providing memorable dining setups for weddings, festivals, and racing days, Sally envisioned a permanent sanctuary on the beachfront.

Sally is a graduate of the prestigious **Ivy Bleu Academy**, where she refined her expertise in wedding design, coordination, and styling. She believes every wedding should be a seamless, stress-free reflection of romance.

"I love love, and there's nothing more beautiful than seeing guests share laughter over beautiful food, with the sound of the ocean waves right next to them."

Sally Owner & Coordinator, Riviera Yeppoon
