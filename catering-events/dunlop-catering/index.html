Our Legacy

Where Woodfire Hardwood Meets Masterful Presentation

For over two decades, Dunlop Catering has defined community feasts and premier celebrations across Central Queensland. We believe in taking the time to cook with purpose, highlighting rich local ingredients and classic techniques.

Our signature mobile spit ovens are fueled by seasoned native hardwoods, slow-smoking selected meats for up to 8 hours to deliver exceptional flavor and melt-in-mouth tenderness.
