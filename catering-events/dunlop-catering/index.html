Since 1998

A Family Tradition of Woodfire & Warmth

For over two decades, Dunlop Catering has been at the heart of community gatherings, backyard celebrations, and dream weddings. We believe in the power of shared meals and rustic cooking techniques.

Our signature mobile spit roasts are slow-cooked over selection hardwoods for up to 8 hours, delivering unmatched tenderness and rich, smoky flavor. Paired with our vibrant, hand-tossed salads and warm cream potatoes, every feast feels like a Sunday family dinner.
