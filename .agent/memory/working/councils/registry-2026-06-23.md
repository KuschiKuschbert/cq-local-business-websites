# Council Registry

- Date: 2026-06-23
- Purpose: route business-engine decisions to the right debate council before action.

- Prospect Research Council (prospecting): New source lane, failed source route, or candidate capture decision. / Gate: Research-only; no account login, contact, forms, DMs, calls, or social interaction.
- Web Design Council (website-design): Private concept, mockup brief, CTA, content structure, or visual direction decision. / Gate: Private concept only; do not publish or imply client approval.
- Offer Pricing Council (offer-pricing): Tier, price, guarantee, scope, CTA, or package decision. / Gate: Offer strategy is not a client promise, invoice, or proposal approval.
- Outreach Review Council (outreach): Drafting, channel choice, follow-up, compliance, or send-readiness decision. / Gate: No send, schedule, DM, form submission, call, social post, or follow-up without explicit Daniel approval.
- Delivery Ops Council (delivery): Client delivery plan, domain, hosting, proposal, handover, or fulfilment sequence decision. / Gate: No hosting, DNS, publishing, billing, or client-facing promises without explicit approval.
- GitHub Work Council (github): Issue draft, branch, PR, remote issue creation, or work orchestration decision. / Gate: No remote GitHub writes without explicit approval.
- Titan CEO Council (strategy): Major prioritization, business model, growth bet, risk tradeoff, or resource allocation decision. / Gate: Strategy recommendations do not approve external actions.
