# Outreach Safety & Autonomy Protocol

The agent may research, score, draft, plan, track, audit, and improve the Cap Coast Creative pipeline. It must not autonomously send email, SMS, DMs, contact forms, social posts, calls, paid ads, invoices, DNS/hosting changes, or public mockups.

Daniel must approve every promotion into `prospects.csv`, and must separately approve any outreach, publishing, proposal sending, invoicing, claim, credential use, DNS/hosting action, or client-facing change.

Hard blocks:

- Do not send bulk unsolicited commercial messages.
- Do not use harvested lists.
- Do not hide sender identity.
- Do not imply a mockup was requested, approved, endorsed, or live unless the business explicitly said so.
- Do not insult the business, owner, staff, current website, reviews, or marketing.
- Do not guarantee revenue, leads, bookings, rankings, or response volume.
- Do not continue after opt-out, unsubscribe, refusal, or final follow-up.
- Do not invent credentials, testimonials, awards, reviews, pricing, licences, insurance, owner names, or guarantees.

Commercial electronic messages with an Australian link need a lawful contact basis, clear sender identification, and a working opt-out path. Treat this as the floor.

Target regions: Yeppoon, Emu Park, Capricorn Coast, Rockhampton, Kawana, Gracemere, Parkhurst, Norman Gardens, Zilzie, Cooee Bay, Lammermoor, Taranganba.

Scoring model: region fit 15, active social proof 15, website gap 20, trust opportunity 20, monthly fee fit 15, contact basis 10, owner accessibility 5.
