# Developer Preferences & Styling Guidelines

These rules dictate design styles, engineering principles, and business settings across our websites.

## 1. Design Aesthetics & Core Principles
- **Design Strategy:** Visual excellence is paramount. The design must feel premium, modern, and high-end.
- **Colors:** Do not use plain/default colors (e.g. standard blue, green, red). Use custom, curated color systems (e.g. HSL tailored colors, sleek dark modes, pastel gradients, subtle glassmorphism).
- **Typography:** Integrate Google Fonts like `Outfit` and `Inter` on every project. Avoid generic system fonts.
- **Interactions:** Implement smooth hover states, transitions, glassmorphic headers, and micro-animations for button presses.
- **Assets:** Never use broken links or simple text placeholders for images. Generate photorealistic custom placeholder images and copy them to the `public/` directory.

## 2. Technical Stack
- **Languages:** Use semantic HTML5 and Vanilla CSS/Javascript for maximum speed, simplicity, and ease of distribution.
- **Frameworks:** Keep configurations frameworkless. Do not use TailwindCSS or React/Vite complex routing unless explicitly requested.
- **SEO & Structure:**
  - One `<h1>` tag per page.
  - Descriptive page titles and meta description tags.
  - Unique, descriptive IDs on interactive components.
  - Highly optimized Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) performance.

## 3. Business Strategy & Pitch Rules
- **Pricing Tier:** All landing page mockups use the **"$0 Upfront, Flat Monthly Fee"** model.
- **Tiers:**
  - Cafés, Salons, Lawn Care: **$199/mo** (e.g. Coffee & Co, Studio Bohemia, Lawn Ranger).
  - Electricians, Plumbers, Cleaners, Pest Control: **$249/mo** (e.g. Keppel Bay Plumbing, Budget Electrical CQ, Bonds Termite).
  - Excavation, Landscaping, Specialty: **$299/mo** (e.g. Mal Earthworks, Fantastic Landscaping).
