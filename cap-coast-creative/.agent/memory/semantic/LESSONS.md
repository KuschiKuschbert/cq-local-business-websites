# Distilled Semantic Lessons & Target Guarantees

Guidelines and trust hooks learned from constructing Central Queensland business sites.

## 1. Niche Trust Hooks & Conversion Mechanisms

### 🪠 Plumbing & Gas Services
- **Pain Points:** Lateness and hidden call-out fees.
- **Rules:** Every plumbing mockup must feature the **"On-Time or We Pay You $50"** guarantee panel and a **$99 flat-rate diagnostics call-out** form.

### 🐜 Pest Control & Carpet Cleaners
- **Pain Points:** Untrusted technicians in home, pricing surprises.
- **Rules:** Display badges indicating **"Police-Cleared Technicians"**, **"$20M Public Liability Insurance"**, and **"Price-Lock Guarantees"** directly inside or next to the quote forms.

### 🚜 Excavation, Hire & Landscaping
- **Pain Points:** Property damage, mulch messes, travel fees.
- **Rules:** Implement the **"Pristine Site Guarantee"** (waste cleanup), rain-delay reschedule panels, access limit warnings, and utility (BYDA) check verifications.

## 2. Location & Geographic Context
- **Primary Regions:** Rockhampton, Yeppoon, Gracemere, and the Capricorn Coast (Central QLD).
- **Guidelines:** Incorporate regional terms in SEO titles/descriptions and link mockups to local reference pages (e.g. Yeppoon beaches, Capricornia Chamber).
