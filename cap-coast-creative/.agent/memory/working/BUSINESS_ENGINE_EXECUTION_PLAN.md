# Cap Coast Creative Business Engine Execution Plan

## Why This Exists

Build a supervised business engine that helps Daniel find real local website opportunities around Rockhampton, Yeppoon, Emu Park, Kawana, and the Capricorn Coast.

The engine should research, score, plan, draft, track, and improve the business. It must not contact people, publish work, charge money, change hosting, or write to external systems unless Daniel gives exact approval for that action.

## Current Position

- Dashboard exists and is owner-readable.
- Local approval buttons exist for approve / hold / reject.
- Five promotion-ready leads are currently on hold.
- Approved prospects are zero.
- Outreach is blocked.
- Safe research is allowed.
- The next research lane is Kawana / Rockhampton hair salon.

## Operating Sequence

1. Research public local business evidence.
2. Capture only businesses with public owned social/profile evidence or an owned website gap.
3. Score lead quality.
4. Put weak evidence leads on hold or cooldown.
5. Prepare private website ideas for strong leads.
6. Ask Daniel for promotion approval.
7. Promote only approved leads into `prospects.csv`.
8. Generate mockup, offer, compliance, and outreach drafts locally.
9. Ask Daniel for exact send approval before any outreach.
10. Review outcomes and improve the system.

## Phase 1: Make Lead Quality Obvious

Goal: Daniel should know whether a lead is strong, weak, held, or not ready.

Done when:

- `lead_quality_map.csv` exists.
- Dashboard shows lead quality in plain English.
- Held promotions are not shown as urgent decisions.
- Weak leads have a clear next research step.

## Phase 2: Build A Strong First Test Prospect

Goal: move one good lead from research into a confident internal offer.

Done when:

- One lead has verified public evidence.
- One private concept exists.
- One offer is clear enough to explain in one sentence.
- Contact compliance still blocks outreach until Daniel approves exact sending.

## Phase 3: Outreach Readiness Without Sending

Goal: prepare but do not send.

Done when:

- Draft message exists.
- Sender identity, opt-out, contact basis, and exact copy are documented.
- Pre-send readiness shows exactly what remains blocked.
- Daniel can approve or reject sending from a clear review surface.

## Phase 4: Supervised First Outreach

Goal: send only after explicit approval.

Done when:

- Daniel approves the exact prospect and exact message.
- Outreach log records the event.
- Replies, opt-outs, and follow-ups have tracking rules.

## Phase 5: Self-Improvement Loop

Goal: make the engine improve from evidence.

Done when:

- Weekly retrospective captures research quality, lead conversion, blocked work, and next experiment.
- Repeated weak searches are suppressed.
- Useful source families are promoted.
- Dashboard keeps showing the next safest business action.
