FAQs & Subscription Architecture

Why is it $0 upfront? What's the catch?

No catch. We want to remove all hesitation. We show you what your site could look like by building a mockup first. If you choose to launch it, we manage the hosting, updates, and domains for a flat monthly fee. This aligning ensures we keep your site modern month-to-month.

Who owns the domain name?

You do. If you have one, we point it to our hosting servers. If you don't, we register it under your business name and configure it fully.

What is included in the monthly fee?

Unlimited updates (text, price changes, service details, team profiles), security certificate monitoring, domain renewals, premium local SEO indexing, and enterprise-grade hosting.

Are there lock-in contracts?

No. We operate month-to-month. If your business changes or you no longer require the site, you can cancel at any time. If you decide to migrate elsewhere, we offer a fair asset buyout plan.

How long does a mockup take?

Once requested, we research your business and deliver a fully interactive demo site link in about 7 business days.
